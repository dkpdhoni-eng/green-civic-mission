Founder’s Message

Greetings.

Today, our country is grappling with a truly dire and grave crisis — the crisis of climate change.

Countless people across the globe are losing their lives because of it, and innumerable animals and birds are falling victim to its effects. The situation has become such that you and I can barely step out of our homes during the summer heat. Other species of living beings are facing extinction.

Should we simply stand by and let this happen?

No — absolutely not; we will not let this continue. We will put a stop to it, for as long as this Earth needs us.

My objective is not to undertake this task alone, but rather to raise awareness among all of you and enlist your support in this mission of mine.

So, join our mission and help save our society — and our planet — from destruction.

- Founder, Green Civic Mission
